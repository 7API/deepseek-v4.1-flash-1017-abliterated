# DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Abliterated FAQ

## What is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Abliterated?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Abliterated is the abliterated variant of the Flash-tier DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 model. Its weights have been modified to reduce the model’s refusal direction while retaining the base model’s knowledge, coding ability and general style.

## What does “1017” mean?

It is part of the build/variant label used for this model. Confirm the exact model ID and availability in the 7API dashboard; do not assume that `1017` is a release date unless the provider confirms it.

## Is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Abliterated the same as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Uncensored?

Not exactly. Abliteration is a weight-level technique; “uncensored” is the broader outcome. A DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 uncensored model may be abliterated, fine-tuned or both.

## Is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Abliterated safe?

It is a tool, not a guarantee. Use it lawfully, evaluate the exact model and add guardrails where output matters. See [Safety and responsible use](./07-safety-and-responsible-use.md).

## Will it never refuse?

No. Abliterated and uncensored variants reduce refusals but do not eliminate them. Behavior depends on the model build, prompt and provider policy.

## Does abliteration reduce quality?

It can have side effects on tone, instruction following and tool use. That is why you should evaluate quality, not just refusal rate.
